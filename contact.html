Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers before you send your message.

How do I advertise my business?

Visit the Advertising page, choose your preferred advertising package, complete the business information and submit your advertising request.

Can I suggest a technology story?

Yes. Select "Technology Tip / News" or "Content Suggestion" in the contact form and provide the relevant information.

Can I contact Wrytole about a partnership?

Yes. Select "Partnership" and provide details about your organization, proposal and what you would like to achieve.

Can I report a problem with the website?

Yes. Select "Technical Problem" or "Website Feedback" and explain what happened, where it happened and, if possible, what you expected to happen.

Will my message be published publicly?

No. A normal contact message is intended for communication with Wrytole and is not automatically published as website content.

What information should I avoid sending?

Never send passwords, mobile-money PINs, banking PINs, verification codes or other confidential account credentials through this form.
