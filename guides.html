Deals & Shopping

How to discover technology deals

The Deals section is designed to help you discover technology products and devices that may be useful for school, business, entertainment, communication, productivity and everyday digital life.

🛍️ What can you do in Deals?

The Deals page allows you to explore available technology products in one convenient place. Depending on what is currently listed, you may find smartphones, laptops, tablets, accessories, networking equipment, storage devices, computer equipment and other technology-related products.

Products may change over time. New products can be added, older listings can be removed and prices or availability can change. Always review the current product information before submitting an order.

1️⃣

Open Deals

Select the Deals section from the website navigation. You will be taken to the technology product listings.

2️⃣

Search

Use the search bar to quickly find a product. You can search using a product name, category, brand or other relevant words.

3️⃣

Review

Read the product information carefully. Check the displayed price, description and other available information.

4️⃣

Add to Cart

If the product is suitable for you, use its Add to Cart button to include it in your order.

🔎 Using the Deals search bar

The search bar is useful when there are many products on the Deals page. Instead of scrolling through every product, enter a word or phrase related to what you want.

✓ Product name: search for a specific device or product name.
✓ Brand: search for a manufacturer or brand shown in the available listings.
✓ Category: try words such as laptop, phone, tablet, accessory or storage.
✓ Keywords: use important words from the product title or description.
✓ Clear search: remove the search text when you want to see the available listings again.

💰 Understanding prices

Technology prices can change because of supplier pricing, availability, exchange rates, promotions, stock levels and other market conditions. The price displayed on the website should therefore be treated as the current listed price at the time you are viewing the product.

Before an order is finalized, TechHub may need to confirm product availability and the final applicable price. This is particularly important when a product has limited availability or when its price has recently changed.

✓ Currency: Product prices are displayed in UGX.
✓ Check before ordering: Review the current price before submitting your order.
✓ Final confirmation: The final price and availability can be confirmed before the order is completed.
✓ Ask questions: Contact TechHub if you need clarification about a product.

Cart & Orders

How to place an order on TechHub

After finding products you are interested in, the cart allows you to review your selections before submitting your order request.

🛒 What is the shopping cart?

The shopping cart is a temporary list of the products you have selected from the Deals page. It allows you to see what you intend to order, change quantities, remove unwanted products and review the estimated order total before continuing to checkout.

Adding a product to the cart does not mean that the order has already been submitted. You still need to review your cart and complete the order form.

Step by Step

Your complete ordering process

Follow these steps when you want to submit a product order request.

1️⃣

Choose a Product

Browse Deals or use the search bar to find the technology product you are interested in.

2️⃣

Add to Cart

Select Add to Cart. The product will be placed in your shopping cart for review.

3️⃣

Review Cart

Open your cart and check the products, quantities, prices and estimated total.

4️⃣

Checkout

Continue to the order form and provide the contact and delivery information requested.

5️⃣

Submit Order

Review the information you entered and submit the order request when everything is correct.

6️⃣

Wait for Confirmation

TechHub can contact you to confirm availability, final pricing, delivery and payment arrangements.

➕➖ Managing products in your cart

Your cart should make it easy to correct your order before submission. Depending on the controls available on the Deals page, you can adjust the quantity of an item or remove an item completely.

➕ Increase quantity: use the quantity control when you want more than one unit of a product.
➖ Decrease quantity: reduce the quantity when you no longer need as many units.
🗑️ Remove: remove an unwanted product from the cart before checkout.
🔄 Review: always check the cart again after changing quantities.

💰 Understanding your order total

The cart may calculate an estimated total using the products and quantities you have selected. This gives you an idea of the value of your order before you submit it.

The displayed cart total should not automatically be interpreted as a final invoice. Product availability, price changes, delivery arrangements or other factors may need to be confirmed before the final transaction is completed.

✓ Product price: the listed price of each selected product.
✓ Quantity: the number of units selected.
✓ Subtotal: the combined value of selected products.
ℹ️ Final confirmation: TechHub may confirm the final price with you before completing the order.

Order Submission

What happens when you submit?

The ordering process is designed to send the details you provide through the website's configured form submission service.

📝

1. Details Collected

The website collects the information you entered together with the products and order information associated with your request.

📨

2. Form Submitted

The completed order form is submitted through the form service configured for the website.

🔎

3. Order Reviewed

The submitted information can be reviewed so that the order can be processed and followed up.

📲

4. Customer Contacted

You may be contacted using the details you provided to discuss the order and confirmation.

⚠️ Important: submitting is not the same as payment

Submitting an order through the Deals page should be understood as sending an order request. It does not automatically mean that payment has been completed.

TechHub can first confirm product availability, final price, delivery or collection arrangements and the appropriate payment process with the customer.

Never send money to an unknown person claiming to represent TechHub without first verifying the payment instructions through the official contact channels provided on the website.

After Submission

What should you expect next?

⏳

Allow Time for Review

Your order may need to be reviewed before someone contacts you. Avoid submitting repeated orders for the same request unless necessary.

📞

Keep Your Phone Available

Keep the contact number you provided accessible so that you do not miss an important follow-up.

💬

Answer Questions

You may be asked to clarify the selected product, quantity, location or another part of your request.

✅

Confirm the Details

Before completing the transaction, verify that the product, price and arrangements are what you expect.

🚨 If your order does not submit

If the website displays an error after you press Submit Order, first check your internet connection and make sure all required fields have been completed correctly.

1. Check that your internet connection is working.
2. Check all required fields for missing information.
3. Review your phone number and email address.
4. Try submitting the form again once.
5. If the problem continues, use the Contact page for assistance.

🔐 Protect your personal information

Only provide information that is reasonably required for your order. Never enter passwords, PINs, banking passwords, verification codes or other highly sensitive security credentials into an ordinary product-order form.

If a person claiming to be a representative asks for a security code, account password or similar credential, do not provide it. Contact TechHub through an official channel to verify the request.

📊 Important advertising expectations

Advertising provides promotional visibility, but no advertising placement can guarantee a specific number of customers, sales, clicks or enquiries.

The effectiveness of a campaign can depend on factors such as the quality of the advertisement, the product or service being promoted, the audience, pricing, competition and customer interest.

Businesses should therefore view advertising as a promotional opportunity rather than a guarantee of sales or revenue.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about using TechHub, ordering products and advertising a business.

❓ Do I need an account to browse Deals?

Not necessarily. The Deals section is designed to allow visitors to explore available technology products. If the website requires specific information before ordering, follow the instructions displayed during checkout.

❓ Can I search for a specific product?

Yes. Use the search bar on the Deals page and enter a relevant product name, brand, category or keyword. The available listings can then be filtered according to your search.

❓ Can I add more than one product to my cart?

Yes. You can add different products to your cart and review them together before proceeding to checkout. You can also adjust quantities where those controls are available.

❓ Does adding something to my cart place an order?

No. Adding a product to the cart only prepares it for your order request. You still need to open the cart, review your selections, complete the order information and submit the order.

❓ Is the displayed product price always final?

The displayed price is the current listed price shown on the website. However, availability and final pricing may need to be confirmed before an order is completed. Always review the current listing and any confirmation you receive.

❓ What happens after I submit an order?

Your order information is submitted for review. TechHub can then contact you using the information you provided to discuss product availability, final pricing, delivery or collection arrangements and payment options.

❓ Is submitting an order the same as making payment?

No. Submitting an order request does not by itself mean that payment has been completed. Wait for the appropriate confirmation and payment instructions.

❓ How can a business advertise on TechHub?

Open the Advertising page, review the available packages, choose the option that suits your campaign and complete the advertising request form with accurate business information.

❓ What advertising packages are available?

The standard options are Basic, Featured and Premium. There is also a Custom Campaign option for businesses that want to discuss requirements outside the standard packages.

❓ How long do the advertising packages run?

📌 Basic: 2 months.
⭐ Featured: 5 months.
🚀 Premium: 7 months.
🎯 Custom: duration discussed according to the campaign.

❓ Does advertising guarantee customers or sales?

No. Advertising is intended to provide promotional visibility, but it cannot guarantee a particular number of customers, enquiries, clicks, sales or revenue.

❓ What information should a business provide?

Advertisers should provide accurate business information, a clear description of their products or services, appropriate contact information and the advertising package or campaign they are interested in.

❓ What if I make a mistake in my order?

If you notice a mistake before submitting your order, return to the relevant field and correct it. If you notice a problem after submission, contact TechHub as soon as possible and provide enough information for your order to be identified.

❓ What if the website does not submit my form?

First check your internet connection and make sure all required fields have been completed. If the problem continues, try again later or contact TechHub through the available contact channel.
