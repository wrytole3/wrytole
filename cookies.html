Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide analytical insights to website owners.

2. How Wrytole Uses Cookies

At Wrytole, we use cookies and similar browser tracking tools to ensure our platform operates smoothly when you explore product deals, submit order requests, or interact with business promotions. Specifically, we use cookies to:

  • Maintain basic site security and prevent fraudulent form entries.
  • Remember your active session or form data when submitting product orders or advertising inquiries.
  • Understand how visitors navigate our tech guides and deal listings so we can optimize performance.
  • Ensure payment checkout redirection (such as merchant payment options) operates correctly.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify the cookies on Wrytole into the following categories:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for core site functionality. Without these, features like submitting forms or processing checkout requests cannot function properly.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us measure traffic patterns, identify popular technology topics, and detect technical errors across the site.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences (such as language or display settings) to deliver a personalized experience on return visits.
  • Advertising & Third-Party Cookies: Set by third-party integrations (such as form submission services or embedded media) to track interaction with sponsored deals and banner promotions.

4. Third-Party Services

Some interactive elements on Wrytole rely on external services (e.g., third-party form processors, embedded videos, or payment collection tools). These services may set their own cookies on your device. Wrytole does not directly control these third-party cookies; we encourage you to review their respective privacy and cookie policies.

5. How to Control and Delete Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to submit order requests or interact with key features on Wrytole.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy periodically to reflect changes in technical requirements, service providers, or site features. Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
