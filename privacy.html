Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Wrytole. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit order requests for technology products, or interact with our business advertising services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when using Wrytole. This includes:

  • Order & Request Information: Full name, contact number, email address, delivery preferences, and product inquiries when you submit product orders or request deals.
  • Business & Advertising Details: Contact information, business name, service details, and promotion specifications provided when registering or inquiring about advertising opportunities.
  • Communication Data: Any information provided through contact forms, feedback forms, or email correspondence.
  • Technical Data: Standard internet log information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction data captured via cookies and analytical tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Facilitating product order requests and connecting you with availability and deal updates.
  • Processing advertising inquiries and managing partner business listings.
  • Responding to user inquiries, customer support requests, and feedback.
  • Improving website functionality, user experience, and digital technology guide offerings.
  • Ensuring platform security and preventing fraudulent transactions.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following necessary circumstances:

  • Fulfillment Partners: Sharing product request details with trusted sellers, merchants, or delivery partners to complete your order requests.
  • Service Providers: Using third-party infrastructure (such as form submission systems, secure database management, and hosting providers) to operate the platform efficiently.
  • Legal Requirements: Disclosing information if required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect the safety, rights, or property of Wrytole or our users.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no internet transmission or storage security mechanism is 100% secure.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Wrytole may use basic cookies and tracking mechanisms to enhance navigation, analyze site traffic, and deliver personalized deal recommendations. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of the data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
